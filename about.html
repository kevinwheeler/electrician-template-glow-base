About Hawking Electrical

Powering Austin homes and businesses with honest, professional electrical work.

Our Story

Hawking Electrical was founded with a simple mission: deliver safe, reliable electrical work at a fair price. What started as a one-person operation has grown into a trusted team serving residential and commercial clients across the Austin metro area.

We believe every home and business deserves an electrical system that's safe, efficient, and built to last. That means doing the job right the first time — no shortcuts, no surprises. Our electricians are fully licensed, bonded, and insured, and we stand behind every project with our workmanship guarantee.

Whether you need a simple outlet repair or a complete panel upgrade, we bring the same level of care and professionalism to every job. That's why our clients keep coming back — and keep referring us to their neighbors.
